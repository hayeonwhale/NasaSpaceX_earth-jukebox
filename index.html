Boundary currents

Western boundary currents are narrow, fast rivers of warm water on the west side of each ocean, carrying tropical heat toward the poles. Pick one and the cursor rides along it while the music reads the map: the pitch falls as the water cools, and the music grows more intense where the flow is fastest.

Map and video: NASA Perpetual Ocean 2, ECCO2 ocean model, 2022 to 2023 (SVS 5479, SVS 5425). The paths are long-term averages; the real currents meander around them.
